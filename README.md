## Simon Heppner

Studying Informatik at TU Wien. I like public transport and the data behind it (GTFS, routing, timetables), simulations and evolutionary algorithms, and building small tools for things that annoy me. I got into programming by making games, and still enjoy starting a new idea and then doing something else entirely after a short while.

**Currently**

Working on Diana GreenConnect and LastMile at Zuugle Services, with occasional contributions to related projects outside.

- tiss-tuwel-cli: TISS and TUWEL in one terminal. Deadlines, grades, course files.

- uebung-merger: puts the Angabe and the handwritten Lösung side by side, ready for the projector.

Most of the older repos here are pygame projects from school. I've had a soft spot for
